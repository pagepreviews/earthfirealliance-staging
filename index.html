Earth Fire Alliance is a global nonprofit coalition building the technological infrastructure needed to detect, monitor, and understand Earth's wildfires — and delivering critical data to the firefighters and scientists who need it most.

Firesat

Built with and for the global wildfire community.

Developed in close collaboration with 200+ firefighters, incident commanders, and scientists, the FireSat constellation delivers frequent, high-resolution data at the speed and scale fires demand.

Testimonials

Why our work matters, in their words.

“As fire seasons get longer and extreme events become more common, it is vital we have access to accurate and timely detection capabilities so we can best protect our communities. Through innovations such as FireSat we can ensure that our firefighters have the best chance to deal with fires while they are smaller. This will mean safer firefighters and safer communities.”

Peter McKechnie, AFSM
DEPUTY COMMISSIONER, STRATEGIC CAPABILITY, NEW SOUTH WALES RURAL FIRE SERVICE.

“Globally, wildfires are increasing in size and severity, impacting lives, communities, critical infrastructure, and watersheds at alarming rates and at significant costs. Earth Fire Alliance will fundamentally change our management approach and response to wildfire.”

Christopher Anthony
FOUNDER & CEO, FIREWERX; FORMER CAL FIRE CHIEF DEPUTY DIRECTOR.

“New data streams are going to be revolutionary for fire incident management and what's shareable in near real-time. And, globally consistent troves of new data for nearly every fire on Earth will create a chronological record of wildfire and related shifts on the planet. This is so important for science and research to help us understand fire behavior as wildfire regimes change.”

Genevieve Biggs
PROGRAM DIRECTOR, WILDFIRE RESILIENCE INITIATIVE AND SPECIAL PROJECTS, THE GORDON AND BETTY MOORE FOUNDATION.
