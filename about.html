FireSat

An advanced satellite constellation designed specifically for wildfire detection, monitoring, and response. FireSat will generate an unprecedented dataset on wildfire and its effects on the planet and our communities.
Register Data Interest
Find FireSat Orbit Visualization

Built with and for the global wildfire community.

For decades, firefighters and scientists have relied on satellites designed for other purposes — meaning critical data that arrives too late and a scientific record that hasn’t kept pace with the scale of the challenge. Earth Fire Alliance was formed to close that gap, and together with our technical partner Muon Space, we developed FireSat differently.

Through interviews with 200+ firefighters, incident commanders, and scientists worldwide, we built what the community asked for: frequent, high-resolution data delivered in time to act. The result is a constellation unmatched by any existing or planned satellite system, and a dataset poised to transform how humanity understands, prepares for, and responds to wildfire.

Six Bands. One Complete Picture.

FireSat’s 6 multispectral imaging bands work together to reveal fire characteristics at high spatial resolution — simultaneously measuring vegetation condition, surface temperature, and fire dynamics in ways no other space-based system can match.

RED NIR SWIR MWIR1 MWIR1A LWIR

Two MWIR Bands = Zero Blind Spots

A traditional single midwave infrared (MWIR) band can oversaturate when capturing extremely hot fires — like an overexposed photograph. FireSat has a unique second MWIR band that is tuned down to capture precise temperature measurements at the highest intensities.

Data Products

.01
Hotspot
Identification

Catch fires early. FireSat provides near real-time detection of new or growing ignitions to identify small, low-intensity fires before they escalate into major threats.

.02
Fire
Perimeter

Know exactly where the fire is. A continuously updated common operating picture of the fire’s outer boundary enables safer, more efficient resource allocation and coordination.

.03
Fire
Progression

Track how the fire moves. FireSat delivers situational awareness of spread, direction, and speed, which is critical for first responder safety, incident command decision-making, and evacuation planning.

.04
Fire
Radiative Power

Measure the fire’s intensity. By quantifying the energy a fire releases, FireSat enables researchers and land managers to assess ecosystem impacts, estimate carbon emissions, and understand fire behavior with unprecedented precision.
